FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a finance or commerce background?

No. The course starts from zero: what a share is, how the market works and how to open a demat account.

Is the course online or offline?

Both. Join classes in person or attend live online. Every class is recorded so you can revise later.

How much money do I need to start trading?

None during the course. You practise on paper first. After that, start small with money you can afford to lose.

Will you give buy or sell tips?

No. This is an education course. We teach you to analyse and plan trades yourself.

Can I pay the fee in instalments?

Yes. Ask us on WhatsApp or during the demo and we'll share the instalment options.
