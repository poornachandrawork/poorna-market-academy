Questions

Before you book.

I know nothing about the stock market. Is this for me?

Yes. Week 1 starts with what a share is and how to open a demat account. Most students in each batch are complete beginners.

How much money do I need to start trading?

None during the course. You practise with paper trading first. When you start with real money, begin small with an amount you can afford to lose.

Will you give buy or sell tips?

No. This is an education course, not an advisory service. We teach you how to analyse and plan your own trades.

Can I learn alongside a full-time job?

Yes. Weekend and evening batches are available, and every live class is recorded so you can catch up.

Will I make money after the course?

Nobody can promise that, and you should avoid anyone who does. We teach a process that protects your capital and helps you make decisions on your own.

What happens in the free demo?

A one-hour class with the mentor. You'll see how we read a chart and plan a trade, and you can ask anything about the course, fees and timings.
