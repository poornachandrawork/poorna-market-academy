The syllabus

Eight weeks, in the order you'll actually need it.

Each week builds on the last. You open a demat account only after week 2, and you don't touch options until your paper-trading journal shows discipline.

01Week 1

How the market works

Exchanges, brokers, depositories and what really happens when you click Buy.

  • NSE vs BSE
  • Demat & trading a/c
  • Order types
  • T+1 settlement
02Week 2

Reading a company

Fundamentals for people who aren't accountants. Enough to avoid obvious traps.

  • P/E & P/B
  • Balance sheet basics
  • Promoter holding
03Week 3

Candlesticks & chart structure

Trends, ranges, support and resistance drawn from real Nifty 50 charts.

  • Candle patterns
  • Trendlines
  • Timeframes
04Week 4

Indicators that earn their place

Moving averages, RSI and volume, and when each one lies to you.

  • EMA 20/50/200
  • RSI divergence
  • VWAP
05Week 5

Risk management

The week that decides whether you survive. Position size, stop-loss and risk-reward on every trade.

  • 1–2% rule
  • Stop placement
  • Drawdown math
06Week 6

Swing & positional setups

Three repeatable setups with entry, exit and invalidation rules written down.

  • Breakout retest
  • Pullback to EMA
  • Range trading
07Week 7

Futures & options basics

Lot sizes, margins, premiums and the Greeks, taught with the risks stated up front.

  • Calls & puts
  • Delta, theta
  • Expiry behaviour
08Week 8

Psychology & your trading plan

Journaling, reviewing losses, and leaving with a written plan you'll follow.

  • Trading journal
  • Revenge trading
  • Plan template

Questions

Before you join

I have zero knowledge of the stock market. Can I join?

Yes. Week 1 starts from what a share is and how to open a demat account. Most of our students are complete beginners.

How much money do I need to start trading?

None during the course. You practise with paper trading first. After that, start small with money you can afford to lose while you're still learning.

Will you give buy/sell calls or tips?

No. This is an education course. We teach you how to analyse and plan trades on your own.

What language are classes in?

Classes are taught in simple English with Hindi and Kannada explanations where helpful.

What if I miss a live class?

Every live class is recorded and available in your dashboard within 24 hours.

Is there a refund policy?

Attend the free demo class first. If you join and leave within the first 7 days, we refund the full fee.
