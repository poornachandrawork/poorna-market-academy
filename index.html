01

Buying on tips

02

Ask on WhatsApp
I know nothing about the stock market. Is this for me?

Yes. Week 1 starts with what a share is and how to open a demat account. Most students in each batch are complete beginners.

How much money do I need to start?

None during the course. You paper-trade first. When you move to real money, start small with an amount you can afford to lose.

Will you give buy or sell tips?

No. This is an education course, not an advisory service. We teach you to analyse and plan your own trades.

Can I do this alongside a job?

Yes. Weekend and evening batches are available, and every live class is recorded.

Will I make money after the course?

Nobody can promise that, and you should avoid anyone who does. We teach a process that protects your capital and lets you decide on your own.

What happens in the free demo?

A one-hour class with the mentor. You'll see how we read a chart and plan a trade, and you can ask about fees, timings and batches.
